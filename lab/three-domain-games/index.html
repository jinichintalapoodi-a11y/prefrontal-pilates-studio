Prefrontal Pilates Studio · Private concept lab

New Ways to Grow

Three independent cognitive game prototypes — not part of the active pilot and not a clinical test.

Prototype 1 · Cognitive flexibility and rule maintenance

Word Weave

Sort a word using the current rule. The rule changes as you play: shift your attention without losing track of what the word means.

Prototype 2 · Verbal learning and free recall

Memory Garden

Study a short group of words. After a brief distractor task, type back the words you remember in any order. Spelling support is intentionally conservative.

Prototype 3 · Visuospatial planning and flexible problem solving

Garden Paths

Plan your route from the blue start to the green garden gate. Walls block your path. Try to solve each garden in a few deliberate moves.

No account, analytics, network transmission or saved scores. Game feedback describes task performance only; it does not measure brain health.
